Total control

/market bakery in new york — scrape an OSM business market → Excel + CSV
/addsearch <avito-url> — save a search to the schedule
/scrape — run all searches right now
/leads /stats — top leads & pipeline numbers

/mini — open the Mini App  ·  /export — CSV dump
Get started

Yours in 3 steps.

From zero to hot leads in your Telegram.

Get a bot token from @BotFather, put it in .env, run docker compose up -d.

02.

Pull your map: send /city Moscow to the bot. Every address gets verified against real buildings.

03.

Feed it sources: add the bot as admin to channels, save Avito searches with /addsearch. Hot leads arrive + open /mini.
